## 🤝 Launch & Utility Partner for Robinhood Chain Projects

Sherwood Labs works as a **technical group for teams launching tokens and building utility on Robinhood Chain**. We take projects from idea to mainnet:

- **Token launch engineering:** contract design, launchpad integration, liquidity setup, and launch-day execution
- **Utility development:** dapps, dashboards, bots, and integrations that give a token real use
- **Smart contract & hook development:** Solidity, Foundry, and custom Uniswap v4 hooks
- **Trading & analytics infrastructure:** market data, alerting, and automation tailored to your token
- **Security-first delivery:** dry-run modes, simulation, and pre-signing transaction review built in
